Emergency notice

This website is not for emergencies. If you or someone with you has severe difficulty breathing, chest pain, coughing up blood, bluish lips, or is confused or collapsing, call an ambulance on 123 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not wait for a clinic appointment and do not use the website forms or email for urgent problems.

Medical disclaimer

The information on this website is general information about the clinic, its doctors, its services and how to prepare for a visit. It is not medical advice, it is not a diagnosis, and it does not replace a consultation with a qualified doctor who has examined you.

  • Do not start, stop or change any medicine or inhaler because of something you read on this website. Discuss it with your doctor.
  • Reading this website or contacting the clinic through it does not create a doctor–patient relationship. That begins only when you are seen in a consultation.
  • The breathing exercise on the website is a simple relaxation aid. Stop if you feel dizzy or unwell, and do not use it in place of treatment.
  • Clinic hours shown on the website are the doctors’ sessions. Appointment times must be confirmed by phone.
  • Images on the website that are illustrations (for example lungs and airways) are for decoration and explanation only.

We try to keep the website accurate and up to date, but we cannot guarantee that every detail is complete or current at all times.

Privacy policy

Who we are. Breathe Easy Clinic (“the clinic”, “we”), THE CLINICS, Clinic 311, third floor, South 90th Street, Fifth Settlement, New Cairo, Egypt. Contact: info@breatheeasyclinic.com.

What we collect through the website.

  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address, your name if you choose to give it, the page language and the date you subscribed, together with your consent.
  • Emails and calls you start: whatever you choose to tell us when you email or call the clinic.
  • Information kept only on your own device: the visit checklist ticks and whether you have already seen the intro are stored in your browser (local/session storage). They are not sent to us.

The website has no patient account, no online medical records and no advertising tracking. It keeps simple, anonymous visit statistics (see “Cookies and local storage”). Please do not send medical reports, scans or detailed health information through the newsletter form.

Why we use it. Newsletter details are used only to send clinic news and updates you asked for. Information you send by email or phone is used to answer you and to arrange your visit.

Who processes it for us. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Newsletter sign-ups are delivered to the clinic inbox through a form-delivery service. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows a Google Maps map; these providers may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads, under their own privacy policies. We do not sell or rent your information.

How long we keep it. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. Emails are kept as long as needed to deal with your request and any clinic record-keeping obligations.

Your choices and rights. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or emailing info@breatheeasyclinic.com. You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, in line with Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020) and other applicable law.

Children. The newsletter is intended for adults. A parent or guardian should contact the clinic on behalf of a child.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local and session storage only for convenience features that stay on your device: remembering your visit-checklist ticks and not repeating the intro animation during the same visit. It also stores a random visitor number in local storage so the clinic can count visits anonymously: each page view records that random number, the page, the site language, the website you came from (domain only) and the device type (mobile, tablet or desktop). No names, contact details or cookies are used for this, and the statistics are kept in our database provider Supabase, which may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the service. If your browser sends “Do Not Track”, no visit is recorded. You can clear these items at any time in your browser settings.

The embedded Google Map and Google Fonts are provided by Google, which may use its own cookies or similar technologies under Google’s privacy policy.
